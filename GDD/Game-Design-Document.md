# Royal Rush

## Mobile Social Casino Game Design Case Study

**Genre:** Free-to-Play Social Casino
**Platform:** Mobile
**Design Focus:** Player Engagement, Risk/Reward, Progression, and Virtual Economy
**Project Type:** Game Design Concept & Case Study

---

## 1. Game Overview

**Royal Rush** is a fictional free-to-play mobile social casino game concept built around a simple slot-based gameplay loop with an additional player-choice mechanic.

The core experience is designed for short play sessions. Players use virtual coins to spin, receive rewards, build streaks, and unlock progressively better features.

The key design feature is a **Bank or Risk** decision. When players reach certain streak milestones, they can either secure a guaranteed virtual reward or risk that reward for the opportunity to receive a larger bonus.

The objective is to introduce a lightweight decision-making layer into a traditionally simple slot gameplay loop while maintaining a clear and accessible mobile experience.

> **Note:** Royal Rush is a fictional design concept using virtual currency only. It is not intended to model real-money gambling.

---

## 2. Design Goals

The design focuses on five primary goals:

### 2.1 Simple Core Loop

The basic gameplay should be understandable within the first few interactions.

**Spin → Reward → Progress → Bonus → Continue Playing**

### 2.2 Meaningful Player Choice

The Bank or Risk mechanic gives players a simple decision without requiring complex controls or rules.

### 2.3 Short-Term and Long-Term Progression

Players should receive immediate feedback from individual spins while also working toward streak milestones, unlocks, and longer-term progression.

### 2.4 Balanced Virtual Economy

The virtual currency system should provide enough rewards to support continued play while maintaining meaningful progression goals.

### 2.5 Iterative Design

The concept should be designed to support playtesting. Player feedback and game metrics can be used to identify confusing mechanics, reward imbalances, or pacing problems.

---

## 3. Target Player

Royal Rush is designed for **casual mobile players who prefer short gameplay sessions** and enjoy simple progression systems.

The intended player experience is:

* Easy to understand
* Quick to play
* Reward-focused
* Visually clear
* Progression-oriented
* Suitable for short sessions

The design avoids requiring extensive tutorials or complex controls so that the player can understand the main gameplay within the first few minutes.

---

## 4. Core Gameplay Loop

The primary gameplay loop is:

**SPIN → RECEIVE REWARD → BUILD STREAK → UNLOCK BONUS → MAKE CHOICE → PROGRESS → SPIN AGAIN**

### Example Session

1. The player starts with virtual coins.
2. The player spends coins to perform a spin.
3. The result provides a virtual reward.
4. The result contributes toward the player's streak.
5. Reaching a streak milestone unlocks a bonus.
6. The player chooses between **Bank** or **Risk**.
7. The resulting reward contributes toward progression.
8. The player continues playing or returns later.

The loop is intentionally short so that individual sessions can be completed within a few minutes while progression provides a reason to return.

---

## 5. Streak System

The Streak Meter provides a short-term progression goal.

Example:

| Streak | Reward / Feature            |
| ------ | --------------------------- |
| 1      | Standard reward             |
| 2      | Small bonus                 |
| 3      | Increased reward multiplier |
| 4      | Bonus opportunity           |
| 5      | Premium virtual reward      |

The exact values would be tuned through balancing and playtesting.

The purpose of the streak system is not simply to increase rewards, but to give players a visible short-term objective during a session.

---

## 6. Bank or Risk Mechanic

When a player reaches a designated bonus milestone, they receive a choice:

### BANK

The player accepts a guaranteed virtual reward.

**Example:**

> Guaranteed reward: 1,000 virtual coins

### RISK

The player enters a short bonus interaction where the potential reward can be higher.

**Example:**

> Potential reward: 0–2,000 virtual coins

The decision is presented clearly so that players understand the potential outcome before making their choice.

### Design Purpose

The mechanic introduces:

* Player agency
* Risk/reward decision-making
* Additional excitement
* A memorable feature within the core loop

The mechanic would require playtesting to determine whether the decision feels meaningful without creating frustration or unnecessarily slowing down the session.

---

## 7. Initial Design Questions

The concept would be tested around several questions:

1. Do players understand the Bank/Risk decision immediately?
2. Is the reward difference large enough to make the choice meaningful?
3. Does the streak system provide a clear short-term goal?
4. Does the bonus interaction interrupt the core gameplay flow?
5. Are rewards frequent enough to maintain engagement?
6. Does progression feel achievable during normal play?

These questions will guide the balancing and playtesting stages of the project.
