# Royal Rush — AI-Assisted Design Workflow

## Purpose

AI tools were considered as design-support tools throughout the concept development process rather than as replacements for human game-design decisions.

## 1. Ideation

AI was used to generate alternative concepts for:

* Bonus mechanics
* Progression systems
* Reward structures
* Player challenges
* Short-session gameplay ideas

The final concept was selected and refined based on simplicity, player clarity, and compatibility with the intended mobile experience.

## 2. Design Critique

AI was used to challenge the initial concept by identifying:

* Potentially confusing mechanics
* Unclear player choices
* Possible reward imbalances
* Excessive complexity
* Alternative player flows

These outputs were treated as suggestions for further design consideration.

## 3. Economy Support

AI was used to help identify variables that could affect the virtual economy, including:

* Spin cost
* Average rewards
* Bonus frequency
* Progression requirements
* Reward pacing

The resulting economy was structured and evaluated using Excel.

## 4. Playtesting Support

AI was used to generate potential playtest questions and edge cases, helping create a structured testing plan.

## 5. Documentation

AI-assisted drafting was used to organize design ideas into a structured GDD and supporting documentation.

## Human Design Decisions

Final decisions regarding gameplay mechanics, player flow, progression, and design priorities remained human-led.

The goal of the workflow was to use AI to accelerate repetitive and exploratory tasks while retaining human judgment over the player experience.
