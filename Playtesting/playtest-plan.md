# Royal Rush — Playtesting & Iteration Plan

## 1. Objective

The purpose of playtesting is to determine whether players understand the core gameplay loop, progression system, and Bank/Risk decision without requiring extensive instructions.

## 2. Key Questions

* Can a new player understand the core loop quickly?
* Is the Streak Meter easy to understand?
* Do players understand the difference between Bank and Risk?
* Does the bonus decision feel meaningful?
* Is progression easy to understand?
* Are rewards perceived as sufficiently clear and motivating?

## 3. Test Flow

A basic playtest session would follow:

1. Introduce the player to the Home screen.
2. Allow the player to begin without detailed instructions.
3. Observe the first several spins.
4. Observe whether the player notices the Streak Meter.
5. Observe the player's response when the Bank/Risk decision appears.
6. Ask the player to explain what they believe each option does.
7. Collect feedback after the session.

## 4. Example Metrics

Potential metrics include:

* Session duration
* Number of spins per session
* Streak completion rate
* Bonus activation rate
* Bank vs Risk selection rate
* Progression completion rate
* Player-reported clarity
* Player-reported enjoyment

## 5. Example Iteration

### Observation

During an initial conceptual review, the Bank/Risk mechanic could potentially introduce unnecessary complexity if the difference between the two choices is not immediately visible.

### Design Change

Make the guaranteed reward and maximum potential reward visible directly on the two buttons.

### Reason

Players should understand the consequence of the decision without opening another information screen.

### Expected Result

A clearer decision-making experience with less friction during the bonus interaction.

## 6. Future Testing

A larger playtest could compare alternative reward values and interface treatments to determine which version produces better comprehension and engagement while maintaining a balanced virtual economy.
